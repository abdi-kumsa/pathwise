# Model comparison (DOC1 step 4)

Generated by `scripts/model_report.py` from `ml/models/ethiopia_comparison.json`. Do not edit by hand.

Every Ethiopia forecast target is scored on a **rolling-origin backtest**: from each origin year (2019 onwards) every candidate forecasts 1–5 years ahead using only data up to that origin, and is scored against what was then observed. There is no random split.

## Candidates

| Candidate | What it does |
|---|---|
| `seasonal_naive` | Last observed value (baseline) |
| `historical_mean` | Mean of the series up to the origin (baseline) |
| `drift` | The series' own linear trend, extrapolated |
| `pooled_ridge` | One ridge model shared by every series of a target: the change from the last value, from the latest change and the distance from the series' mean |

The request path uses, at each horizon, the candidate with the lowest backtest RMSE. The choice is recorded on every forecast row as `selected_model`.

Prediction intervals are the 5th–95th percentiles of the selected candidate's backtest residuals at that horizon. Their coverage is measured leave-one-origin-out: the percentiles from all other origins are applied to the held-out one.

## Derived targets

These DOC1 targets are computed from the estimated ones, not forecast separately.

| Target | Derived from | Method |
|---|---|---|
| `M2.employment_growth` | `M2.employment_demand` | (forecast employment / last observed employment − 1) × 100 |
| `M2.occupational_demand` | `M2.vacancies` | vacancy × occupation share of the region's vacancy mix — not a copy of the vacancy point |

## What these numbers mean

Errors measure how well each estimator recovers the simulated generator's process, not Ethiopian reality. They become ministry-relevant only when the same backtest runs on official series.
